# Security Roles Overview

Security roles in this solution define what users can see and do within the system. Each role is designed to match a specific job function or responsibility, ensuring users have the right level of access to perform their work efficiently and securely.

## 1. Auto Allocation Leader
- **Purpose:** For users who lead or oversee the auto-allocation of tasks or cases.
- **Key Capabilities:**
  - Can read, create, and update user status records.
  - Has global access to SharePoint data and documents.
  - Can read system plugins and configuration.
- **Typical Users:** Team leaders or supervisors responsible for managing automated task allocation.

## 2. Auto Allocation User
- **Purpose:** For users who participate in the auto-allocation process but do not have leadership responsibilities.
- **Key Capabilities:**
  - Can read and update their own user status.
  - Has global access to SharePoint data and documents.
  - Can read system plugins and configuration.
- **Typical Users:** Staff members who are assigned work through auto-allocation.

## 3. Rules Administrator
- **Purpose:** For users who manage routing rules and keywords within the system.
- **Key Capabilities:**
  - Full control (create, read, update, delete, assign, share) over routing rules and keywords.
  - Can manage queues and SharePoint data.
  - Can read and configure web forms and system plugins.
- **Typical Users:** Administrators responsible for configuring and maintaining business rules.

## 4. Service Leader
- **Purpose:** For users who lead service teams and require broad access to records and configuration.
- **Key Capabilities:**
  - Can create, assign, and manage activities, contacts, notes, queues, and workflows.
  - Has access to email templates and SharePoint integration.
  - Can activate workflows and manage user settings.
- **Typical Users:** Service managers or team leads overseeing casework and team performance.

## 5. Standard User
- **Purpose:** For general users who need to work with records but do not require administrative privileges.
- **Key Capabilities:**
  - Can create, read, and update activities, contacts, notes, and queues.
  - Can use email templates and SharePoint integration.
  - Has basic workflow and notification capabilities.
- **Typical Users:** Most staff members using the system for day-to-day work.

## 6. Team Leader
- **Purpose:** For users who lead teams and need additional privileges beyond standard users.
- **Key Capabilities:**
  - Can create, assign, and manage activities, contacts, notes, queues, and email templates.
  - Has access to workflow and SharePoint integration.
  - Can manage team records and settings.
- **Typical Users:** Team leaders responsible for managing a group of users and their work.
